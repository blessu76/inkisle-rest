In Act 24 of Inkisle, an island-conquest strategy game in development, a flag marks an island worth thinking about before you send a fleet. The act is one of 30 acts and 240 stages in Season 1. We’re developing the game for iOS.

## What the flag tells you

While you hold a flag island, you can see enemy fleets and strike warnings through the fog. The rule uses the island's current owner. If you lose your last flag island, you lose that view. Before sending out a fleet, you can weigh taking another island against keeping this one.

## Two reasons to go to the same island

An island can be valuable because of where it sits or because holding it tells you what is moving. The flag makes both reasons matter on the same board. Seeing a fleet gives you something to act on while it is still on the way. You still have to decide where to send your own.

## FAQ

### What changes when I capture a flag island?

Enemy fleets and strike warnings become visible through the fog for as long as you hold a flag island.

### Does that view stay after I lose the island?

The view lasts while you own a flag island. If the last one changes hands, that view goes with it.
