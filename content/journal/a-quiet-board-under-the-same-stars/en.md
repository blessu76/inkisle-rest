Act 26 of Inkisle is called The Same Star. The game is still in development, and Season 1 comprises 30 acts and 240 stages. Here we wanted the other side of the sea to look as if it were under the same sky. We gave stage 26-4 a quieter board, where you can focus on taking all the neutral islands. We’re developing the game for iOS.

## A board you can turn around

The islands are laid out in symmetry. In the stages with one opponent, turning the positions halfway around the centre matches each island to its counterpart. In the stages with two opponents, the left and right sides mirror one another. You can start by reading the shape of the board with the island types you already know.

## A pause in the middle

Stage 26-4 asks you to take all the neutral islands. It gives you a single goal to follow midway through the act. The later boards ask more of that familiar layout, with a line of islands to cross and, at the end, two allied opponents. The pause has a place in the sequence, much like a short beat between two lines of a story.

## FAQ

### Does Act 26 introduce a new sea rule?

No. It uses familiar island types and changes the layouts and objectives.

### What is the goal in stage 26-4?

Take all the neutral islands.
