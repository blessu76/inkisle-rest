### Comments and likes
Journal articles on ipe.rest can have a like button and a comment box. You don't need an account.

**What we collect when you comment**
- the **nickname** you type (up to 30 characters) and the **comment** text (up to 500 characters);
- the **language** of the page you were reading (en, ko, ja, …) and the **time** you posted;
- a **random browser token** — a string of letters and digits generated in your browser; it is not linked to your name, e-mail or IP address, and it is used only to enforce a short pause between two posts and to spot repeated abuse;
- a **report count** for the comment, increased when readers press "Report".

Your nickname, comment, language and posting time are **shown publicly** under the article, in every language version of it. Please don't put personal information in them. Links are not allowed.

**What we collect when you like or report**
- a like only increases a counter for the article; we store no identity with it;
- a report only increases the report count of that comment.
In your browser's local storage we keep small markers so the page remembers that you liked, reported, or just posted (and the random token above). They never leave your browser except the token described above, and you can clear them in your browser settings.

**Where it is stored.** Comments, like counts and report counts are stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), in the Seoul region (asia-northeast3). Google acts as our service provider. Google may receive your IP address and device information when your browser contacts its servers, as described in Google's privacy policy.

**Moderation.** Comments are not reviewed before they appear. A comment that receives **3 reports** is hidden automatically, and we may hide or delete any comment (for example for abuse, spam, personal information or links).

**How long we keep it.** A comment is kept for as long as the article is published, and at most **{{RETENTION_YEARS}} years** after it was posted, unless you ask us to delete it earlier or we remove it. Hidden comments are deleted within **{{HIDDEN_DELETE_DAYS}} days**. Like and report counters carry no personal data and are kept as long as the article.

**Delete your comment.** E-mail **ipe.haven@gmail.com** with the article address and the nickname and (a few words of) the text you wrote; we delete it within **{{DELETE_REQUEST_DAYS}} days**. We can't identify a comment from anything else, because we don't keep your name or e-mail. We will reply to confirm.
